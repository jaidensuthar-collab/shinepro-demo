# Neighbor tech jobs

## How to send a job (phone or computer)

1. Open **claude.ai/code** in a browser, or the **Code** tab in the Claude app.
2. Pick the `shinepro-demo` repo.
3. Type the job in plain words, e.g. *"She needs a printable guide for video-calling her grandkids on her iPad."*

Claude logs the job in `jobs/`, does the work, puts files for her in `deliverables/`, and pushes it. Open the files on GitHub (web or the GitHub app) to download, print, or forward them.

## Layout

- `client.md` — her devices, accounts, preferences (no passwords)
- `jobs/LOG.md` — every job at a glance, handy for billing
- `jobs/` — one file per job
- `deliverables/` — what she receives, one folder per job
